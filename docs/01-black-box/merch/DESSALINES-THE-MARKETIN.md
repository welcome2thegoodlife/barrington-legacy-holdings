## 1. Overview

DESSALINES | THE MARK:ET:IN is a historical figure merchandise collection in the BLH Black Box™ Universe, honoring Jean-Jacques Dessalines, a leader of the Haitian Revolution who declared Haiti's independence in 1804.

The collection follows the Black Box™ merchandise principle: merchandise should emerge from the story rather than interrupt it, and it should extend historical education, never replace it.

## 4. Historical Lineage & Attribution

Per Governance section 9, modern derivative work identifies both the historical lineage and the modern creator.

**Historical figure:** Jean-Jacques Dessalines.

**Historical lineage of the artwork:** The central pose (sword raised in one hand, a severed head held in the other) is adapted from Manuel López López's 1806 etching "Desalines," published in Mexico City in *Vida de J. J. Dessalines, gefe de los negros de Santo Domingo*, a Spanish translation of Louis Dubroca's 1804 French biography. Source: [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/vidadejjdessalin00dubr).

**Context:** That book is a colonial-era source whose full title emphasizes the "atrocities" of the revolution's leaders. The original image was made by a source hostile to Dessalines. This makes the collection's "Tell The Real Story" message important: the product should be paired with a short, sourced history of who Dessalines was and why the image exists.

**Evidence tier (source image origin):** Tier 1, documented publication.

**Rights note:** The 1806 original is public domain by age. Confirm the terms of any specific scan or reproduction used as reference.

**Modern creator:** BLH Black Box™ Universe. Designer credit: *to be recorded.*
