Contributing to Barrington Legacy Holdings

Welcome.

Barrington Legacy Holdings is being built as an open, documented ecosystem where people can help turn ideas, research, creative work, technology, education, and community knowledge into durable projects.

The Core Loop

RECOVER → VERIFY → ATTRIBUTE → EVALUATE → CREATE → PROTECT → RETURN → TEACH → REPEAT

Ways to Contribute

People can contribute through:

- Historical research and source verification
- Archival discovery and documentation
- Story development, animation, music, film, and media
- Software, hardware, AI, and Creator Cube prototyping
- Business models, financial modeling, and market research
- Product, brand, and commerce development
- Education and curriculum design
- Community history and oral-history stewardship
- Legal and intellectual-property research
- Testing, editing, design, accessibility, and documentation

Evidence Standards

Research should clearly distinguish between:

Tier 1 — Verified Fact

Supported by strong primary documentation and/or multiple reliable sources.

Tier 2 — Strongly Supported

Credible evidence exists, but additional verification is still useful.

Tier 3 — Contested

Credible sources disagree or attribution remains unresolved.

Tier 4 — Working Hypothesis / Oral Memory

Preserved as a lead, cultural memory, or research hypothesis. It must not be presented as established fact without further verification.

Do not upgrade a claim simply because it fits the desired story.

Attribution & Credit

Contributors should identify sources, creators, collaborators, historical lineages, and communities whenever known.

Modern derivative work should not erase the people or communities whose knowledge inspired it.

Intellectual Property

Placing material in this public repository does not automatically transfer ownership.

Unless a specific license, assignment, or agreement says otherwise, contributors retain their applicable rights in their original contributions.

Projects may require separate legal agreements for:

- Copyright
- Trademarks
- Patents
- Licensing
- Royalties
- Commercialization
- Descendant or community interests
- Confidential or proprietary information

Sensitive Information

Do not publish private medical, genomic, financial, identifying, or otherwise sensitive personal information.

Where appropriate, use privacy-preserving summaries, permissions, hashes, or references to controlled records.

Proposals

For substantial ideas, open an Issue or submit a Pull Request with:

- What problem does this solve?
- Which project or workstream does it belong to?
- What evidence or prior work supports it?
- What would a successful first version look like?
- Who needs to be credited or consulted?
- What risks, rights, or dependencies should be considered?

Working Principle

The record is preserved. The understanding can evolve. The audit trail remembers both.

Why We Build

The purpose of this repository is bigger than storing files.

We are building a system that other people can understand, contribute to, improve, build upon, and carry forward.

The mission must be bigger than the founder.

The goal is to create something capable of continuing beyond any single person while keeping intention, attribution, evidence, creativity, ownership, and human impact at the center.

Thank you for helping build the legacy.
